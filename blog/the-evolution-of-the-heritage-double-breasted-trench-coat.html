The Evolution of the Heritage Double-Breasted Trench Coat

By Sartorial History Curator • 7 Min Read

Originally commissioned for military officers in World War I, the **Double-Breasted Trench Coat** remains a timeless outerwear staple. Its iconic details—storm flaps, epaulettes, and D-ring belts—blend utilitarian weatherproofing with elegant tailoring.

1. Gabardine Weave Innovation

Invented in 1879, tightly woven gabardine cotton naturally repelled rainwater before synthetic coatings existed, providing breathable storm protection.

2. Modern Wool & Cashmere Tailoring

Today's luxury trench coats elevate classic silhouettes with double-faced Italian wool-cashmere blends and real buffalo horn closures.
