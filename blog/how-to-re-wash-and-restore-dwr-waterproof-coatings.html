How to Re-Wash and Restore DWR Waterproof Coatings

By Outerwear Maintenance Specialist • 6 Min Read

Over time, dirt, body oils, and abrasion cause water droplets to flatten out rather than bead up on technical jacket shells (known as "wetting out"). Laundering and heat activation restore your **Durable Water Repellent (DWR)** coating.

1. Technical Wash Cycle

Wash your hardshell in warm water using a specialized residue-free tech wash cleaner. Avoid standard household detergents or fabric softeners that clog membrane pores.

2. Low Heat Activation

Tumble-dry the clean jacket on low heat for 20 minutes. Heat melts microscopic DWR polymers back into upright hydrophobic alignment across face fabrics.
