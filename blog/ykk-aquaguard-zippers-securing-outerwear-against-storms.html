YKK AquaGuard Zippers: Securing Outerwear Against Storms

By Hardware Systems Engineer • 6 Min Read

Zippers are traditionally the weakest entry point for wind-driven rain on waterproof hardshells. **YKK AquaGuard Zippers** feature a polyurethane film laminated to zipper tape coils that completely seals out moisture without heavy storm flap overhangs.

1. Polyurethane Lamination Barrier

The glossy or matte PU film forms an airtight seal when teeth mesh together, preventing water penetration even under high wind pressure.

2. Lightweight Streamlined Construction

By eliminating bulky fabric storm flaps, AquaGuard zippers reduce total jacket weight and improve zipper glide fluidity.
