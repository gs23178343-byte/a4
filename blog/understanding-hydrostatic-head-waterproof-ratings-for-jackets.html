Understanding Hydrostatic Head Waterproof Ratings for Jackets

By Technical Textile Director • 7 Min Read

**Hydrostatic Head (HH)** is the universal measure of fabric waterproofness. Expressed in millimeters (mm), it indicates the height of a liquid water column a fabric membrane can withstand before water droplets penetrate through the weave.

1. Waterproof Rating Tiers

While 10,000mm resists light rain, expedition-grade jackets require at least 20,000mm to 28,000mm HH ratings to withstand heavy driving rain under backpack shoulder strap pressure.

2. Breathability & Moisture Vapor Transmission (MVTR)

Expedition membranes balance high waterproof ratings with exceptional MVTR breathability (20,000g/m²/24h), venting internal sweat vapor to prevent inner condensation.
